# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Obsidian Git Vault aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, technically
rigorous community for users and contributors regardless of background,
identity, or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate and constructive. Focus on code, reproducible evidence, and
technical trade-offs. Respect contributors' time, credit others, accept
constructive review, and protect private user data.

Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers may
moderate discussions, remove abusive content, close threads, or restrict
participation. Enforcement is proportionate to the reported behavior.
